Meet String Bean!

This is String Bean, a purebred Minnesota Couch Hound. That's not a real breed, we made it up because she loves lying around on the couch when she is home alone. She knows she isn't supposed to, but she does it anyway

String Bean likes canned food. Like, a lot. If we try to feed her dry food and don't at least spoon a bit of canned food onto it, she will make faces at us like the one she's making here.

String Bean is also kind of a jock. She loves to run and run, and she is very fast. She is also kind of a hunter… she has killed two rabbits in our yard this past summer. We DO NOT encourage her to do this, but we also can't stop the stupid rabbits from coming into the yard. (Also, they eat plants in the garden!)

  • WoofDorf Astoria in Racine, WI: Where String Bean stays when her parents abandon her to travel out of town.
  • Blue Buffalo Homestyle Beef: String Bean's favorite canned food.
  • Ruff Start Rescue in Minnesota, where we adopted String Bean.
